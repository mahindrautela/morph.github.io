Abstract

MORPH is a modality-agnostic foundation model for spatiotemporal physics. A single convolutional vision-transformer backbone processes heterogeneous 1D, 2D, and 3D PDE data with different spatial resolutions and scalar or vector fields.

The model converts each mini-batch to a Unified Physics Tensor Format and combines component-wise convolution, field-wise cross-attention, and axial attention. MORPH improves transfer learning across modalities, scales effectively from 7M to 480M parameters, and produces stable autoregressive forecasts.

Architecture

MORPH maps heterogeneous observations to a common representation, reasons over spatial and temporal structure, and decodes the next physical state.

1Unified Physics Tensor Format

Represent time, fields, components, and spatial axes consistently.

2Component-wise convolution

Extract local structure from every physical component.

3Field-wise cross-attention

Exchange information between related physical fields.

44D axial attention

Reason along time, depth, height, and width independently.

5Autoregressive decoder

Predict the next state and roll forward in time.

X ∈ ℝB×T×F×C×D×H×W
